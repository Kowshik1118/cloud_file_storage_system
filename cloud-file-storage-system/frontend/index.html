Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upload files to my CloudVault storage?

You can drag and drop any file directly onto the dashbaord drop zone, or click the "+ Upload" button in the topbar to browse files from your device.

How do I upgrade my storage plan?

Click the top-left menu (three dashes ☰), select Membership, choose Pro or Enterprise plan, and click "Upgrade". Your limit updates instantly in real time.

How do I reset my password if I forgot it?

On the Sign In page, click "Forgot password?", enter your email address to receive a 6-digit code, and set a new password.

Are my uploaded files secure?

Yes, all stored files are segregated by user account using MongoDB Atlas GridFS storage with secure authentication tokens.
